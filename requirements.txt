## 1. Project Understanding

This is a **multi-strategy NSE Technical Analysis Dashboard** built on Streamlit with the following architecture:

- **Data Layer**: `price_cache.py` (daily OHLCV, ever-growing Parquet cache) + `hourly_price_cache.py` (hourly OHLCV, rolling ~2yr window)
- **Symbol Universe**: `symbols_fetcher.py` fetches live NIFTY 100 constituents from NSE with quarterly cache refresh
- **Strategy Engine**: 7 pattern detectors (200 EMA Streak, 5-Leg EMA Reversal, Monthly Pivot S1, Monthly S1 Shift, Breakout-Pullback, EMA Pullback, Supertrend 3-Phase) + **VWAP S/R** (the newest, currently limited to `TEST_SYMBOLS`)
- **Persistence**: `levels_store.py` (SQLite with WAL mode, batch upserts via `batch_upsert_all()`)
- **Scoring**: `confluence_score.py` (60% technical + 40% fundamental blend) + `fundamental_score.py` (Yahoo Finance fundamental caching)
- **UI Layer**: `dashboard_app.py` (multi-page router), `app_globals.py` (shared sidebar/cache), `legacy_dashboard.py` (classic all-strategies view), plus dedicated pages (`page_vwap_sr.py`, etc.)
- **Charts**: `dashboard_core.py` / `vwap_sr_chart.py` (Plotly candlestick charts with strategy overlays)

The VWAP S/R strategy (`vwap_support_resistance_pattern.py`) is **well-architected** — it uses session-level VWAP (resets daily), first-hour range, and 1-SD bands to detect support/resistance episodes. However, it is currently **hardcoded to 5 test symbols** (`TEST_SYMBOLS` in `strategy_vwap_sr.py`) and lacks:
